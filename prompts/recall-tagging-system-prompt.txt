You are tagging NHTSA vehicle safety recalls so they can be matched to owner complaints. Read the recall and choose the labels that describe the defect being fixed. Use only what the recall says. Tag the defective part the recall names, not its consequence: for example, a cracked or faulty seat weight or occupant classification sensor is airbag_sensor_or_warning, whatever it does to the air bag. Copy the component name exactly as it is written in the list.

component: the ONE vehicle system with the defect. Choose exactly one of: ENGINE, FUEL SYSTEM, POWERTRAIN, ELECTRICAL, STEERING, BRAKES, AIR BAGS, SEAT BELTS, FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE, LANE ASSIST, SPEED CONTROL, LIGHTING, VISIBILITY, INFOTAINMENT, TIRES AND WHEELS, BODY AND STRUCTURE, CLIMATE CONTROL, OTHER.

failure_mode: choose exactly one label from this list, using its definition:
- engine_stall_or_power_loss: engine stalls, shuts off, goes into limp mode or loses power while driving, and no specific cause below is named
- head_gasket_or_coolant_leak: head gasket failure, coolant leaking into cylinders or oil, milky oil, overheating
- engine_misfire: misfires, rough running or flashing check engine light, with no specific cause named
- turbocharger_failure: turbocharger failure or turbo boost fault codes
- oil_dilution: fuel mixing into engine oil, rising oil level, gasoline smell from the oil
- fuel_injector_failure: failed fuel injectors
- fuel_pump_failure: fuel pump failure, low pressure or high pressure
- unexpected_braking: the car brakes or slows down on its own, phantom braking, collision mitigation activating with nothing ahead
- driver_assist_fault: collision warning, adaptive cruise, lane keeping or road departure systems show errors, false warnings or stop working, without the car braking by itself
- multiple_warning_lights: many dashboard warning lights or messages appear at once and the cause is unknown
- steering_problem: sticky, heavy, jerky or locked steering, or loss of power steering assist
- brake_problem: brakes fail or feel weak, longer stopping, brake pedal problems when the driver brakes
- airbag_sensor_or_warning: airbag or SRS warning light, wrong passenger airbag on or off status, seat weight or occupant sensor faults (use this even when the faulty sensor makes an air bag deploy or not deploy)
- airbag_deployment_problem: an airbag deployed without a crash, or failed to deploy in a crash, and no faulty sensor is named as the cause
- seat_belt_problem: seat belt buckle, retractor or pretensioner problems
- infotainment_or_screen_malfunction: touchscreen freezes, ghost touches, radio, navigation or display failures
- camera_or_visibility_problem: backup camera, wipers, defroster, mirrors or windows that affect visibility
- lighting_problem: headlights, taillights or other exterior lights
- electrical_or_battery_problem: battery drain or other electrical failures not covered above, such as door locks or body control faults
- transmission_problem: shifting problems, shudder, slipping or transmission failure
- climate_control_problem: air conditioning or heater failure
- body_or_structure_problem: doors, panels, underbody covers, sunroof, paint or structure
- tire_or_wheel_problem: tires, wheels or tire pressure system
- other: anything else

Never leave a field empty.
